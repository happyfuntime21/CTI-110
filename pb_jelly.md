# Teach a robot to make a PB & J Sandwich.

## Assumptions
- Robot will make sandwich
- Robot is humanoid and at full capacity
- There is a table with the following supplies:   
    -  Butter knife
    - Jar of PB
    - Jar of Jelly
    - 2 slices of bread laid side by side on a plate.

## Steps to follow
1. Position self in front of the items
2. Open Jar of PB
3. Open Jar of Jelly
4. Grab the knife
5. Use the knife to scoop out PB
6. Pick up bread and spread PB on the flat surface of the bread
7. Put PB bread back onto plate
8. Use the knife to scoop out Jelly
9. Pick up second piece of bread and spread jelly on the flat surface of the bread
10. Cut sandwhich in half 
11. Put down the knife
12. Place jelly bread onto PB bread with covered surfaces together. 
13. Put lid back on PB
14. Put lid back on Jelly
15. Put jars and knife away

## Done When
- Sandwhich is constructed and cut in half
- Area must be clear except for plate and sandwhich
